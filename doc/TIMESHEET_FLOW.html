Superseded, 05-Aug-2026. The status and flag model has been simplified since this was written. There is now one set of six statuses used at every level — Not yet submitted, Submitted, Approved, Rejected, Defaulted, Overridden and approved — with no separate scheme for a day, a week or a month. “Closed” has been removed (month confirmation is an event recorded in OC_TS_MONTH_CONFIRM, not a status), and salary hold is out of scope for now. Status and flags are now shown in a single diagram.

Timesheet Flow

The whole lifecycle of one employee-week, and the only two cut-offs that affect it. Missing either — or both — produces Defaulted.

01  The two cut-offs

Only two matter to a timesheet

Cut-offWhoseMissing it produces
WeeklyEmployee Defaulted with DEFAULTED_BY = 'EMPLOYEE'. The week auto-submits with default hours and locks — only a manager can then edit it. This is the one that holds salary.
DeliveryManager Defaulted with DEFAULTED_BY = 'MANAGER'. Not locked — the manager can still approve it late. This one must never hold the employee's salary.

The other five cut-offs in Period Control — Finance, Book, MEC, Client and Payroll — drive downstream finance and payroll, not timesheet status.

03  Worked example

Reject, then resubmit — before and after the cut-off

Week 3 of July 2026 runs Mon 13 to Sun 19. The weekly cut-off is Monday 17:00, so for this week that is Mon 20-Jul 17:00. Both cases end in the same status; only the flag differs.

In both cases the week returns to the manager. And in both cases the Resubmit row is what records that this was a correction — which is exactly why the Correction flag was safe to drop.

04  Open

One decision the chart cannot settle

Can a Defaulted week be confirmed to accrual?

RULE-020 gates month confirmation on every employee being Approved. A Defaulted week is not, so as drawn it blocks the month. But advance close says defaulted hours are “treated as approved”, and they are meant to reach accrual as ENTRY_TYPE = 'Default'.

Recommendation: keep it blocking, with advance close as the explicit exception — because the alternative lets default hours reach revenue with no human decision at all.
