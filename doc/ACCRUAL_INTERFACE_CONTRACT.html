O2C Timesheet Module Interface contract for Accrual21 Aug 2026

Timesheet hours, at three grains

What the timesheet module hands over when a project month is confirmed: three views on one snapshot, the REST endpoints that serve them, and the one thing the accrual side is allowed to write back.

1Start here

The three views are empty until a month is confirmed. They are not a live read of timesheets — they are a view over a snapshot that only confirm_month writes. A query for period_month = 8 returns zero rows today, and will keep returning zero until a manager confirms August on that project. That is correct behaviour, not a broken feed: nothing is owed to accrual until somebody has signed the month off.

Two consequences worth designing around. Zero rows is ambiguous — it means either "not confirmed yet" or "confirmed with no hours", and those need different handling on your side. Check OC_TS_MONTH_CONFIRM (exposed at GET accrual/confirmed/:periodId) to tell them apart. And a month appears all at once, per project, at the moment of confirmation — there is no partial or trickling state to poll through.

You already have direct SELECT. O2C_TIME granted it to O2C_DEV on all three views, so the queries you sent work as they are. The endpoints in §4 exist for the OIC flow, which cannot open a database session.

2The three views

All three roll up from the same rows, so their hour totals are identical and only the row count changes. Pick by what you are posting.

Which one to use
ViewOne row perUse it to answer
v_oc_ts_anx_month person × project × month What do we accrue for this person this month?
v_oc_ts_anx_day person × project × day Which days make that number up?
v_oc_ts_anx_day_task person × project × day × task × entry type Which task, and was this line a correction?
The totals must agree across all three. They derive from one grain, so a difference is a bug in our views rather than a rounding artefact — please report it rather than reconciling around it.

Two project codes travel together, deliberately. PROJECT_NUMBER is Fusion's and MAIN_PROJECT_NUMBER is yours. Join on MAIN_PROJECT_NUMBER; the Fusion one is there so a disagreement can be reconciled without a third lookup. It cannot arrive null — as of 21 Aug 2026 a project with no link to your side is refused at confirmation rather than handed over unkeyable, so you never have to name-match around a missing code.

4Endpoints

Base: <ords-host>/o2c_time/oc/time/admin/. All responses are ORDS collection feeds — {"items":[…],"hasMore":…,"offset":…,"limit":…}. Honour hasMore and page; a project month at the finest grain runs to a few hundred rows and the default limit will truncate it.

GET accrual/summary/month/:periodYear/:periodMonth

One row per person per project month. Optional: ?projectNumber= ?mainProjectNumber=

GET accrual/summary/day/:periodYear/:periodMonth

Tasks collapsed. Optional: ?projectNumber= ?mainProjectNumber= ?employeeId=

GET accrual/summary/daytask/:periodYear/:periodMonth

Finest grain, including corrections. Same optional filters as /day.

POST accrual/summary/ack/:confirmId

Acknowledge everything under one confirmation. Body: {"status":"Y","message":"…","actor":"ACCRUAL"} — Y stored, E rejected. Returns {"confirmId":…,"batchesAcknowledged":…,"rowsAcknowledged":…}.

Already available, and still the right tool for some jobs

Existing endpoints
MethodPathPurpose
GETaccrual/confirmed/:periodIdWhich project-months are confirmed — use to tell "no rows" from "not confirmed"
GETaccrual/pull/:periodYear/:periodMonthRaw unprocessed rows at the finest grain, with BATCH_ID
POSTaccrual/ack/:batchIdAcknowledge one batch
GETaccrual/extract/:confirmIdEverything for a single confirmation
Two acks, one mechanism. ack/:batchId pairs with pull; summary/ack/:confirmId pairs with the summary views, which do not expose BATCH_ID. The confirmation-level ack resolves to every batch underneath and calls the same routine, so a month with a later correction posted against it is fully acknowledged rather than partly. Use one or the other consistently.

5Reading the hours

Hours are signed, so you can always SUM(). Reversal rows are negative; everything else is non-negative. This is a database constraint, not a convention — you never need sign logic, and a SUM over any grain gives the net position directly.

Leave hours are apportioned, not whole days. A person 25% allocated to a project who takes one 8-hour day off contributes 2.00 leave hours to that project, not 8.00. The remaining 6 belong to their other projects. Do not gross leave back up to a full day.

6What you may write

Hours are not writable. There is no endpoint that changes a figure, and that is deliberate. The hours here come from approved timesheets, and a confirmed month is frozen — that is the entire reason this is a snapshot with the names copied onto it rather than a set of joins back to our live tables. A number you have already posted against must stay the number you posted against.
Ownership
FieldOwnerYou can
PROCESSED_FLAGAccrualwrite via ack — N → Y or E
PULLED_ONAccrualwrite stamped by ack
PROCESS_MESSAGEAccrualwrite your message on ack
All hour columnsTimesheetread only
All name / id columnsTimesheetread only
ENTRY_TYPE · FLAGTimesheetread only

Acking with status = "E" is how you reject a batch. It marks the rows and stores your message, and it does not alter any figure — it tells us to look, so we can correct at source.

7When a figure is wrong

Corrections arrive as a pair of new rows, never an edit. A retro adjustment approved after its month was confirmed writes a Reversal for the old figure and an Adjustment for the new one, and both are carried across by a top-up run. 

Original   2026-07-14  T-100  Actual       8.00
Reversal   2026-07-14  T-100  Reversal    -8.00
Adjustment 2026-07-14  T-100  Adjustment   6.00
                                        -------
net                                        6.00
  • The month is never restated. The original row stays exactly as you first read it.
  • Corrections carry the same CONFIRM_ID as the month they correct, on a later BATCH_ID.
  • They can arrive after you have acked the month. Re-poll a confirmed month, or poll accrual/pull for unprocessed rows.
  • Post the delta, not the difference you compute. Both lines are given to you explicitly.
ACTION_DATE vs WORK_DATE on corrections. A retro adjustment carries the date the correction was decided, not the day worked, so it lands in the period it was agreed in rather than reopening a closed one. WORK_DATE still says which day the hours belong to.
